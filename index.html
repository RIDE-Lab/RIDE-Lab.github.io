System boundary

Engines execute. The control plane decides.

RIDE is not another inference engine. It is the policy, coordination, and evidence layer that turns workload intent and live telemetry into bounded actions on an engine fleet.

03 / INTENTAgents, workflows, users

DAG state, deadlines, quality targets, budgets, reuse signals, and service policy.

02 / RIDEInference control plane

Observe, predict, route, admit, place, budget, recover, and verify.

01 / EXECUTELLM inference engines

vLLM-HUST, vLLM, SGLang, and compatible serving backends execute prefill, decode, cache, and communication paths.

00 / RESOURCEAccelerators, memory, network

The data plane remains independently measurable and replaceable.

Boundary rule

A RIDE mechanism must be expressible as a decision over observable state. Engine patches are allowed only as minimal reusable telemetry or actuation seams; kernels, KV transport, compilation internals, and hardware backends do not become control-plane work merely because they expose a flag.
